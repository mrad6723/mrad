Knowledge Base & Common Inquiries

Instant answers to frequently encountered deployment questions.

How long does DNS propagation take for my domain? ▾
Most record updates (A, CNAME, TXT) propagate within 15 to 30 minutes across global resolvers, though full worldwide propagation can take up to 24–48 hours depending on TTL values.
Where do I manage MX and SPF records for email? ▾
MX and SPF authentication records are configured directly inside the Domains registry control panel at domains.mrad.co under your domain's DNS zones.
How do I reset my MRAD ID password? ▾
Navigate to account.mrad.co and click "Send Password Reset to Email" under the Security section, or use the reset form if you are currently logged in.
Can I link custom third-party nameservers? ▾
Yes, custom NS records (such as Cloudflare, Route53, or private DNS) can be specified on any active domain registered through MRAD Domains.
